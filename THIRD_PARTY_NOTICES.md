## career-ops (reference only)

The Workable, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, BambooHR and Breezy job-board adapters in
`src/jobai/discovery/sources/`, and a portion of the starter company list in
`config/companies.yaml`, were informed by the public provider notes and seed list of
career-ops (https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops), used as a reference for which
public endpoints exist and what their payloads look like. The code here is an independent
Python re-implementation; no career-ops source is copied or executed. Every endpoint and
every company slug added from it was re-verified live before being enabled.
